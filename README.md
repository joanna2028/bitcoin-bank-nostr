
# Bitcoin Bank

Bitcoin Bank turns posts into payments on Nostr + Lightning, creating a simple path from social activity to creator income.

## What it is

Bitcoin Bank is a Bitcoin-native social finance project.

It connects:
- posts
- payments
- creator income

The core idea is simple:

**Posts → Payments → UBI**

## Why it matters

Today, social platforms generate enormous value, but creators often capture very little of it.

Bitcoin Bank explores a cleaner model where value can move directly through Bitcoin rails, with instant settlement and a more sovereign user experience.

## Project vision

Bitcoin Bank is designed as a practical Bitcoin-native layer for social payments and creator monetization.

The long-term vision is to make social value flow directly into Bitcoin, in a simple and usable way.


## Background

This project sits within a larger research direction around Bitcoin-native governance, identity, and digital finance.
